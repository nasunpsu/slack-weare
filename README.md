# WeAre!
> A reseasrch project to connect distance learners enrolled in university degree programs.

<!-- [![NPM Version][npm-image]][npm-url]
[![Build Status][travis-image]][travis-url]
[![Downloads Stats][npm-downloads]][npm-url] -->

WeAre! is both a SlackBot and a community space for learners enrolled in PSU courses to engage with their peers and be part of the community. More broadly, the WeAre! Concept stems from a research project aimed to connect learners across distance and build up their collective capacity as a community of learners. Thanks to the participation of numerous World Campus instructors and students, researchers from the College of Information Sciences and Technology at Penn State have found a lot about what a community of World Campus is for its members, using a mix of research methods, through a series of studies.
